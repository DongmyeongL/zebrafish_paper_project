# Drosophila FC Analysis Method

This document records the final functional-connectivity analysis used for
Figure 15. The current standard follows
`figure15_drosophila_final/code/figure15_drosophila_full_combined_FINAL.py`.

## Final Analysis Unit

The selected FC workflow is **Branson999-first**. Functional measures are
computed on the full Branson999 ROI atlas whenever feasible, and aggregation to
matched FlyWire/SC regions is performed only at the final SC-FC comparison
stage.

This choice avoids losing ROI-level functional heterogeneity before computing
FC/FCV. It also matches the final Drosophila SC-FCV comparison logic: FCV is
estimated at the Branson ROI level first, then summarized into side-aware
matched regions/blocks for comparison with FlyWire Post-DCA+.

The previous matched41-first heatmap, in which ROI traces were averaged into
region mean traces before computing all FC measures, should be treated as a
QC/sensitivity version rather than the final primary FC workflow.

## Sliding-Window FC and FCV

The final FC analysis uses:

- window: 30 frames;
- step: 8 frames;
- sampling rate: 1.2 Hz;
- TE discretization: 4 quantile bins;
- TE lag: 1 frame.

For each recording, Branson999 ROI traces are z-scored and pairwise Pearson
correlations are computed in sliding windows. For each ROI pair, FC is the mean
window correlation, and FCV is the standard deviation of the window correlation:

\[
FC_{ij} = \langle r_{ij}(w) \rangle_w,
\qquad
FCV_{ij} = \mathrm{SD}_w(r_{ij}(w)).
\]

ROI-level FCS and FCV are then defined by averaging over ROI pairs, excluding
the diagonal. For SC comparison, these ROI-level values are aggregated into
matched regions or side-aware blocks at the final step. In the preferred
SC-FCV comparison, FCV for a matched region/block excludes ROIs belonging to
the same side-aware key before averaging.

## Metastability

Metastability is computed within each matched region using the ROI traces
assigned to that region. For each ROI trace, an instantaneous phase is obtained
with the Hilbert transform. Regional phase coordination is computed from the
mean pairwise phase consistency across ROIs, and metastability is the temporal
variance of that coordination signal. Regions containing only one ROI have
undefined metastability and are assigned missing values.

## Net Transfer Entropy

For each recording, matched-region mean traces are discretized into four
quantile bins. Transfer entropy is computed at lag 1 frame for every ordered
region pair:

\[
TE_{i \rightarrow j}
=
H(Y_{t+1},Y_t)-H(Y_t)-H(Y_{t+1},Y_t,X_t)+H(Y_t,X_t).
\]

Net TE is the antisymmetric directed difference:

\[
NetTE_{ij}=TE_{i\rightarrow j}-TE_{j\rightarrow i}.
\]

Region-level NetTE drive is the mean outgoing NetTE across target regions.

## Neighbor Net TE

FC neighbors are determined within each recording using Fisher-z approximate
Pearson p-values with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction at \(q<0.001\).
Neighbor NetTE is the mean NetTE drive of significantly FC-connected neighbor
regions.

## Z-Scoring and Averaging

For each recording, the five region-level measures are z-scored across regions:

1. `FCS_z`;
2. `FCV_z`;
3. `Metastability_z`;
4. `NetTE_z`;
5. `NeighborNetTE_z`.

The final summary table averages these recording-level z-scores across
recordings for each matched region.

## Final Figure 15 Functional Panels

Panel J in `figure15_drosophila_full_combined_FINAL.py` uses all available
Branson999 recording-level ROI points. It reads
`Branson999_full_ROI_5measure_recording_level_w30_step8_FINAL.csv` and merges
ROI labels with major anatomical divisions from
`Branson999_w30_weighted_vs_binary_PostDCApositive_scores_FINAL.csv`.

The displayed J panel therefore uses 8,935 recording-ROI FCV points grouped by
major anatomical division. The current Kruskal-Wallis result is:

\[
H=1663.10,\qquad p<10^{-300},\qquad n=8935.
\]

Panel M uses the 41 side-aware block summary table and displays five row-wise
z-scored functional measures:

1. `FCS_z`;
2. `FCV_z`;
3. `Metastability_z`;
4. `NetTE_z`;
5. `NeighborNetTE_z`.

The heatmap is ordered by hierarchical clustering, with higher FCV-oriented
patterns placed toward the left when possible. Point-count labels are
intentionally not displayed above the J/K group plots in the final figure.
